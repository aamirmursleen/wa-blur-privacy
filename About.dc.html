Privacy forthe screenyou already use.

Keep WhatsApp Web private and organised on your own computer: blur and PIN-lock chats, plus reminders, quick replies, translation and export for the chats you have open.

Privacy

Hide what's on screen

Blur messages, chat previews, contact names, profile photos, media and the typing field, each with its own strength. Press Alt+X to hide everything at once.

Lock

Lock it when you step away

A 6–12 digit PIN, auto-lock when idle, and private chats that stay hidden until you unlock them.

Productivity

Get more done in WhatsApp Web

Reminders, quick replies, a smart inbox, notes, translation into 100 languages, and export to TXT, HTML, CSV, JSON or Excel.

How we build it.

Four rules the extension follows in every version.

01

Chat history stays on your computer

Settings, notes and saved history live in Chrome's storage on your device.

02

No analytics, ads or publisher account

We run no servers for the extension and don't track how you use it.

03

No remote code

All libraries are bundled. Nothing is downloaded and run from a server.

04

It never sends a message for you

Reminders notify you. Every message is sent by you pressing Send.
