How to report

Send copyright notices to hello@wasecret.com with the subject "DMCA Notice" and include all six items listed below.

01

What this policy covers

This policy covers this website and material we publish with the extension, such as the store listing, images and documentation.

The extension does not host user content. Chats stay on each user's own computer, so we cannot remove content from anyone's WhatsApp. For content shared on WhatsApp, contact WhatsApp directly.
02

Filing a takedown notice

Email hello@wasecret.com with the subject "DMCA Notice". Under 17 U.S.C. § 512(c)(3), your notice must include:

1Your physical or electronic signature, as the copyright owner or a person authorised to act for them.
2Identification of the copyrighted work you believe is infringed.
3Identification of the material you believe is infringing, with enough detail to find it, such as a URL.
4Your name, postal address, telephone number and email address.
5A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the use is not authorised by the copyright owner, its agent or the law.
6A statement that the information in the notice is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you are authorised to act for the copyright owner.
03

What happens next

We review every complete notice. If it is valid, we remove or disable access to the material and, where possible, tell the person who published it. Incomplete notices may not be acted on.

04

Filing a counter-notice

If you believe material was removed by mistake or misidentification, send a counter-notice to the same address that includes:

1Your physical or electronic signature.
2Identification of the material that was removed and where it appeared before removal.
3A statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good-faith belief the material was removed by mistake or misidentification.
4Your name, address and telephone number, and a statement that you consent to the jurisdiction of the federal district court for your address (or, outside the United States, any judicial district in which WASecret.com may be found), and that you will accept service of process from the person who filed the original notice.

If we receive a valid counter-notice, we may restore the material after 10 to 14 business days unless the original complainant tells us they have filed a court action.

05

Repeat infringers

Where appropriate, we will stop working with anyone who repeatedly infringes copyright.

06

False claims

Under 17 U.S.C. § 512(f), anyone who knowingly makes a material misrepresentation in a notice or counter-notice may be liable for damages. Please make sure the material is actually infringing before you file.

07

Contact

Copyright notices and questions: hello@wasecret.com. You can also use the contact form and choose "Copyright / DMCA".
