In short

This website does not set analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and there are no ad networks on it.

01

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website saves in your browser. They are often used to remember settings or to track visits across sites. Similar technologies include local storage and tracking pixels.

02

Cookies on this website

We do not set cookies for analytics, advertising or tracking, and we do not use tracking pixels. There are no third-party ad networks on this website.

03

Third-party requests

This website loads its fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com). These requests share your IP address and browser details with Google, under Google's privacy policy.

04

The extension

The WA Blur and Privacy extension does not use cookies. It keeps your settings and local data in Chrome's extension storage on your computer. The Privacy Policy explains what is stored and how to delete it.

05

Managing cookies

You can view and delete cookies in your browser's settings. In Chrome, open Settings, then Privacy and security, then Third-party cookies or Delete browsing data.

06

Changes and contact

If we start using cookies, we will update this page first and, where the law requires, ask for your consent. Questions: hello@wasecret.com.
